From concrete to corneas to code

I started as a civil engineer. Structural thinking led me to biomechanics, and an M.Tech at IIT Hyderabad took me into ophthalmic engineering, where my thesis simulated how suture tension changes the cornea after a transplant.

That work showed me how much of eye care now runs on images. Since then I have focused on AI for retinal imaging: cleaning and auditing datasets so models can be trusted, and segmenting disease in OCT scans across different devices.

Alongside the imaging work I build applied GenAI systems, including a privacy-first RAG assistant, because the same habits matter in both: careful data handling, honest evaluation and clear documentation.

  1. Bangalore Institute of Technology. Foundations in mechanics, surveying and environmental engineering, which later fed straight into biomechanics.

  2. Qualified with a score of 450 and joined IIT Hyderabad.

  3. CGPA 9.18. Thesis: finite element modelling of penetrating keratoplasty in Abaqus and Python, studying how suture tension affects corneal biomechanics.

  4. First industry role, in the medical domain.

  5. RetinaPrep for fundus dataset auditing, and OcuVal for OCT fluid segmentation as a registered RETOUCH challenge team.
